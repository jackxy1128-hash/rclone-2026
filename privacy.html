Privacy policy

Last updated: 2026-10-25

rclone-2026 is used only by its owner, to copy the owner's own files from the owner's computer to the owner's own Google Drive using rclone.

  • Files and Google account tokens stay on the owner's computer and in the owner's Google Drive. Nothing is sent to any other server.
  • The app does not collect, sell, share or analyze any data.
  • The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
